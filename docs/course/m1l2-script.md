So why do we even need agents?
Well — here's the thing.

An LLM can only answer.
Ask it to plan your week, and it'll hand you a perfect plan.
And then? It just stops.

It can't check your calendar.
It can't make the call.
The email simply doesn't get sent.

That's where agents come in.
They close the gap.

The LLM does the planning.
The agent does the doing.
Tools make it real — search, code, APIs.

But one call is never going to be enough.
The agent calls a tool, reads the result, and has another go.

Same loop.
Call. Read. Decide. Repeat.
Until the job's done.

It doesn't stop at an answer.
It actually finishes the job.

So why agents, then?
Because knowing isn't doing.

An LLM hands you a map.
An agent walks the road.
And when it's done, it comes back and tells you.

Next up — where agents already work.
Follow for the full course.
AI Agents Zero to Hero.
