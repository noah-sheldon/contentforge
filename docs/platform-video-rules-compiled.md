# Platform Video Rules — Compiled Reference
> Ready for agent prompts. July 2026.

---

## 1. INSTAGRAM REELS

### Length & Format
- **Target:** 15–90s. <3m for discovery. Hard max 20m (poor distribution).
- **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical (1080×1920).
- **Cadence:** 3–4 Reels/week.

### Hook Rules
- Hook in **first 3s**. No logos, no slow intros, no title cards.
- Must work **sound-off** (most viewers watch muted first).
- Types: visual surprise, question, bold statement, pattern interrupt.

### Text Overlay
- **Safe zone:** Keep top 10% + bottom 35% free of critical text (UI overlap).
- **Captions:** Always add. Use Instagram's CC sticker. Center of screen.
- **Style:** Bold, contrasting colors. White text + black outline/shadow.

### Music & Audio
- Trending audio = algorithm signal for Explore/Feed/Reels tab.
- TikTok trends hit Reels ~2-3 weeks later.
- Duck background music to **~30-40% of voice level**.
- Voiceover must be clearly audible primary layer.

### Hashtags
- **3-5 targeted hashtags** (not 30).
- Funnel: 1 broad + 2-3 niche + 1 branded.
- Counter-intuitive: no hashtags got 23% better reach than overloaded.
- Relevance over popularity.

### Caption Structure
- ~150 chars for punchy; 300+ for storytelling (drives 70% more traffic).
- Structure: Hook → Value → CTA → Line break → Hashtags.
- Emojis ok. Line breaks for readability. End with question for comments.

### CTA Patterns
- Educational: "Save this" / "Tag someone" drives saves/shares (algorithm signals).
- Traffic: "Link in bio" across text + verbal + caption.
- Entertainment/trending: skip CTA.

### Editing Rhythm
- Cut every **2-4s**. Remove all silence. Front-load energy.
- Jump cuts standard for talking-head.
- Speed adjustments: slow for emphasis, fast for transitions.

## 2. TIKTOK

### Length & Format
- **Optimal:** 21-34s (business); 15-30s (general). Max 10min.
- **Algorithm:** Rewards completions + re-watches.

### Hook Rules
- Hook in **first 3s**. Mandatory.
- Re-hook every **5-7s** with visual change, text card, or sound shift.
- Hook types: curiosity gap, contrarian, relatable confession, result tease, pattern interrupt.

### Sound Usage
- Trending audio = categorization signal + higher FYP reach.
- Most trends anchored to specific audio track.
- Original sound adoption = free distribution when others use your audio.
- Voiceover: "Netflix documentary" style or "Carrie Bradshaw" confessional.

### Pacing & Editing
- Jump cuts = default. Remove all pauses, breath gaps, dead air.
- **1-2 cuts/sec** average. Educational: 0.5-1 cuts/sec. Entertainment: 2-3 cuts/sec.
- Beat-synced transitions, reaching-out, iPhone swipe, transition dab.
- Text on screen: 2-4s per card. Max 5 words/line, 2 lines. Sync to sound.
- B-roll: new visual every 3-5s.

### Hashtags & SEO (2025/2026)
- **3-5 hashtags** (CapCut/ByteDance data).
- 3-3-3 Formula: 3 broad + 3 niche + 3 content-specific (up to 9).
- 41% of Gen Z use social apps as primary search engine.
- TikTok cross-references: audio + caption text + visuals + hashtags.
- Keywords in first 1-2 lines of caption MUST match spoken audio keywords.

### Caption Strategy
- Caption text: for SEO keywords, context, hashtags. Short preferred.
- On-screen text: 2-4 words per line, timed to beats/cuts.
- Both critical for TikTok SEO — algorithm scans caption text + audio.

### Trends
- Lifecycle: ~2 week refresh. Prime entry window: 30-100 posts.
- Monitor FYP + TikTok Creative Center for rising sounds/hashtags.

## 3. YOUTUBE SHORTS

### Length & Format
- **Optimal:** 15-30s (95% of top Shorts under 15s). Max 3min.
- Longer Shorts see steep retention drop-off.

### Thumbnail Strategy
- Custom thumbnails optional (desktop only). Edits are final.
- Face close-up + high-contrast background + 3-5 words bold sans-serif text.
- Warm colors (red/orange) outperform cool.
- Max 3 visual elements. No YouTube-red play buttons (blend into UI).

### Description
- First **125 chars** carry most SEO weight. Front-load keyword.
- 2-3 sentences max. 2-3 relevant hashtags.
- Title: max 100 chars, clear about content (no bait).

### Retention Tactics
- Hook in **first 2-3s** — open with most compelling visual or question.
- **Design for the loop** — ending visually/narratively loops back to start.
- Every frame earns its place — no establishing shots, no pauses, no filler.
- Fast cuts (1-3s per shot). Trending audio + voiceover + text overlays outperform silent.
- Completion rate > view count algorithm signal.

## 4. YOUTUBE LONG-FORM

### Length by Type
| Type | Length |
|---|---|
| Simple tutorial | 3-5 min |
| Complex tutorial | 10-20 min |
| Educational deep dive | 15-30 min |
| Vlog | 8-15 min |
| Entertainment | 5-10 min |
| Podcast/Interview | 20-60 min |
| Review/Comparison | 8-15 min |

### Narrative Arc
- Hook in **first 5s** — no logos, no intros, no "hey guys welcome back".
- Promise + preview pattern: "In this video I'll show you X. Here's what you'll learn: [3 previews]."
- Problem → Solution → Result for tutorials. Story circle for vlogs.
- **Interest spike every 60-90s** — visual change, new data point, mini-reveal.
- Early recap for drop-in viewers (~30% arrive mid-video).
- CTA before retention drop-off (~60-70% mark), not at end.

### Chapter Markers
- Must start at 00:00. Minimum **3 chapters** (else not shown in search).
- Each chapter ≥10s. Optimal: 3-10 chapters.
- Titles: descriptive + keyword-rich (appear as Google "key moments").

### Retention Curve
- **20-40% drop-off in first 30s** — make-or-break zone.
- Secondary drop-off at **50-70%** mark — save bonus insight for this zone.
- Pattern interrupt every 90s. Subscriber retention 2-3x longer than non-sub.
- Check YouTube Studio Analytics > Audience retention. Cut/tighten drop-off timestamps.

### End Screen Best Practices
- Last **5-20s** of video. Minimum 20s end screen content required.
- Max 4 elements. **2-3 recommended**: 1 video + 1 subscribe.
- "Best for viewer" video element (YouTube auto-selects).
- Subscribe element in bottom-right (highest mobile interaction).
- Mobile-first design — elements must be large enough to tap.

## 5. LINKEDIN

### Video vs Text
- Video significantly outperforms text. Watch time = #1 algorithm signal.
- LinkedIn Live gets **23x more comments** than standard video.
- 9:16 portrait wins over horizontal.

### Length
- **Sweet spot: 15-30s** (optimal watch time 13-15s).
- Max 10 min native. Under 15s for ads.

### Hashtags
- **3-5 hashtags**. Precision over volume.
- Post-2024: hashtags = SEO signals, not discovery tools.
- Capitalize multi-word: #SocialMediaMarketing (accessibility).
- Mix: broad + niche + branded per post topic.

### Engagement Hooks
- Hook in **first 1-3s** — question, startling stat, or value promise.
- State what's coming explicitly. Attention drops after 10s.
- Ask questions in video + caption.

### Caption Style
- **79% watch muted** → captions required on every video.
- 150-char body recommended.
- Lead with industry keywords (LinkedIn SEO).
- Include a link. Professional tone. One idea per paragraph.

### Best Content Types
- Thought leadership, behind-the-scenes, tutorials, testimonials, product demos, event highlights, LinkedIn Live (Q&A, AMA).

### Timing
- Best: Tue-Thu, 8-9 AM. Weekends avoid. Consistency > volume.

## 6. FACEBOOK

### Community-Building
- Use video inside Groups to spark discussions.
- Feature UGC: ask followers to submit videos, share + credit.
- Respond to all comments. Pair video posts with polls.

### Engagement Tactics
- Hook in **first 3s** — first frame + 3s determine scroll-past vs watch.
- Ask questions in copy. Tag relevant Pages.
- Use Facebook Live (ranks higher + boosts non-live content).
- CTA in copy + mid-roll text overlay + end card.

### Sharing Mechanics
- **ALWAYS upload natively** — native has +477% higher share rate than YouTube links.
- Never paste YouTube links (algorithm penalizes).
- Craft searchable title. Give preview in copy.
- Nudge shares explicitly ("Share this if...").

### Length
- Native video: 30s-3min. Reels: under 90s.
- Live: 10-30min. One key point per video.

### Caption Strategy
- Captions on EVERY video (silent auto-play default).
- Colorful text overlays (not just white).
- Suggest "tap for sound". First line is the hook.
- 3-5 hashtags. Clear CTA in every caption.

## 7. THREADS

### Context
- Text-first, video-second. Meta values "quieter, less video-centric experience."
- Images outperform video (+0.6% engagement over video).
- Don't repurpose long-form content directly. Use short clips (<60s) as accents.

### Reply Culture (#1 Lever)
- Replying to comments boosts engagement **42% per post** (Buffer, 128K posts).
- Algorithm weights: replies > profile visits > time spent > repeat interactions > passive likes.
- Median engagement rate: 6.25% (73.6% higher than X at 3.6%).
- Ask open-ended questions. Replying to others' threads = as effective as original content.

### Thread Density
- 500-char limit per post. Text attachments: up to 10K chars (not search indexed).
- Video limit: 5 min. Photo limit: 10 per post.
- Keep individual posts concise (500 chars forces this). Reserve long text for existing audience.

### Hashtags
- "Tags" = topic signals, not search filters. Use sparingly.
- 200+ official communities (Dec 2025). Tag your niche, don't keyword-stuff.

### Timing
- Posting window: Weekday mornings 6-11am. Best: Thursday 9am.
- Fast growth: 3+ posts/day. Minimum: 1 post/day.

### Tone
- Conversational, raw, casual. Start with a thought, expand in replies.

## 8. AUDIO / SFX BEST PRACTICES

### Mixing Levels (Dialogue-Referenced)
| Element | Level |
|---|---|
| Dialogue (anchor) | -6 dB to -12 dB |
| Music (under dialogue) | -20 dB to -30 dB |
| Music (no dialogue) | -12 dB to -18 dB |
| Sound effects | -10 dB to -20 dB |
| Ambience | -25 dB to -35 dB |
| Audio ducking | -6 to -10 dB when speaker active |
| Final loudness | -14 to -16 LUFS |

### Transition Sound Effects
| Type | Duration | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| Swoosh/Whoosh | 0.3-1.0s | Fast cuts, scene changes |
| Riser | 1.0-3.0s | Building tension toward reveal |
| Impact | 0.2-1.0s | Hard cuts, beat drops, reveals |
| Stinger | 0.5-2.0s | Punchline, hook, logo reveal |
| Reverse cymbal | 0.5-2.0s | Cinematic build-ups |

### Ambient Layers
- Record 30-60s room tone on every shoot. Layer under entire timeline.
- Maintain **18dB gap** between ambient noise and desired signals.
- Use volume automation for ebb and flow.
- Low-pass filter distant sounds. Roll off below 150-200Hz. Reduce 350Hz-2kHz.

### Music Trends by Platform
| Platform | Genres |
|---|---|
| YouTube (long-form) | Cinematic orchestral, lo-fi, ambient |
| TikTok | Trending pop, EDM, hip-hop (energetic, beat-driven) |
| Instagram Reels | Jazz, lo-fi, chill, trending (sophisticated lifestyle) |
| Threads | Lo-fi, chill, conversational indie (low-energy backdrop) |
| Educational | Clear instrumental, optimistic, lo-fi |

### Copyright-Safe Sources
- Paid: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed, Soundstripe, Uppbeat.
- Free: YouTube Audio Library, Clipchamp, platform-native libraries.
- AI: Artlist AI, Suno (simpler licensing, reduced plagiarism risk).

## 9. TEXT OVERLAY & CAPTION BEST PRACTICES

### Font Sizes
| Use Case | Size |
|---|---|
| Primary text (mobile) | 18-24pt / 18-32px minimum |
| Secondary/supporting | 16-20pt |
| Hook/emphasis words | 1.5x larger than filler |
| YouTube subtitles | 24-32px (Roboto or Arial) |
| Instagram Stories | ~20% larger (smaller viewport) |

Best fonts: Helvetica Neue, Open Sans, Roboto, Montserrat, Source Sans Pro.

### Position — Safe Zones (1080×1920 Vertical)
| Platform | Top Margin | Bottom Margin | Left Margin | Right Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 220px | 640px | 120px | 240px |
| Instagram Reel | 110px | 320px | 60px | 120px |
| Instagram Story | 250px | 250px | 60px | 60px |
| YouTube Shorts | 140px | 270px | 70px | 190px |

- **Danger zone:** bottom 20% + right 15% of screen.
- **Gold zone:** center-middle block, slightly above eye level.
- Keep within middle 80% of screen width. 60px min from screen edges.

### Text Timing
| Content Type | Duration per caption | Reading speed |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 1-3s | 220-250 WPM |
| Instagram | 2-4s | 200-240 WPM |
| YouTube | 2-6s | 180-220 WPM |
| LinkedIn | 3-5s | 160-200 WPM |

Universal rules:
- Min display: **1.5s** regardless of length. Max: **6s**.
- Pre-roll: text appears **0.5-1s** before audio begins.
- Post-speech: text lingers **0.3-0.5s** after speech ends.
- Sync tolerance: ±200ms from audio. Fade: 100-200ms.

### Caption Formatting
| Format | Max chars/line |
|---|---|
| Short-form (TikTok, Reels) | 15-20 per line |
| Long-form (YouTube, LinkedIn) | 32 per line max |
| Two-line max | 64 total (32 per line) |

### Contrast Ratios
- Gold standard: white text on black (21:1).
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum: 4.5:1 (7:1 optimal).
- Platform styling: TikTok = white/yellow + black outline + bold chunky font.
- Instagram = bold text + word-by-word highlight + brand colors.
- YouTube = white text + black outline or semi-transparent background.
- LinkedIn = white/light gray on dark, professional.

### Performance Impact
- 80% higher engagement with subtitled videos.
- 12-15% longer watch times with captions.
- 15-25% more shares for subtitled videos.
- 20-40% increase in completion rate with well-designed captions.

## 10. COLOR GRADING BEST PRACTICES

### Platform-Specific Guidelines

**Instagram (Reels & Stories):**
- Slightly less saturated than broadcast (compression artifacts).
- Polished, editorial, aspirational aesthetic.
- 3-5 consistent brand colors across all content.
- High-contrast text (white + black outline) survives compression.

**TikTok:**
- Grade **5-10% cooler and slightly lower contrast** than target — TikTok's compression warms footage and boosts contrast.
- High-saturation combos work (vivid bg + white text, neon on black).
- First 0.5s must grab — vivid gradient overlays (pink-purple, blue-green).
- LUT intensity: 65-75%. Verify on iPhone screen before publishing.

**YouTube:**
- Best compression — supports 4K HDR.
- Master Rec. 709 for most feeds. PQ/HLG for HDR.
- Ensure 9:16 vertical crop brightness matches horizontal source.

### Skin Tone Handling
- Natural skin tones fall along consistent diagonal on vectorscope (I-line).
- **Target:** 20-50% saturation, 40-70 IRE luminance.
- ~50-60% IRE in sunlight, ~50% window light, ~30% bar light.
- Isolate skin with qualifier/power window. Check position on vectorscope.
- **Avoid:** pushing skin into cool tones (looks sickly), over-saturating skin (waxy), heavy teal-and-orange on faces.
- Test every grade on full range of skin tones.

### LUT Workflow
1. Shoot log profile. 2. Color correct (white balance + exposure, match clips). 3. Apply technical LUT (Log → Rec. 709). 4. Apply creative LUT at **65-85% intensity**. 5. Check skin tones + scopes. 6. Fine-tune per clip.

### 2025 Trends
| Trend | Look |
|---|---|
| Hyper-Realism | Natural tones, lifelike, balanced |
| Desaturated Pastels | Wellness/lifestyle — saturation <30%, lifted black point |
| Hyper-Vibrant Neon-Pop | Tech/energy — selective hues at 80-100% saturation |
| Split Toning | Warm highlights + cool shadows |

### Mobile-Conscious Grading
- Design for 9:16 vertical. Central 80% of frame has priority.
- Increase mid-tone contrast for small-screen readability.
- Avoid chroma smearing / color banding (high-saturation degrades faster on mobile).
- Perceived brightness of vertical crop must match horizontal source.
