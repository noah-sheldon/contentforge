# Social Media Video Best Practices (2025/2026)

> Compiled from SproutSocial, Hootsuite, Buffer, Later, and other creator sources.
> Actionable rules for coding into agent prompts.

---

## LINKEDIN

### 1. Video vs Text Performance

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| VIDEO > text for engagement | Video posts significantly outperform text posts. LinkedIn algorithm favors video. | SproutSocial |
| LinkedIn Live dominates | Live videos get **23x more comments** than standard video posts. | SproutSocial |
| Watch time = key signal | Watch time is the most important algorithm signal — retention drives reach. | Buffer |
| 9:16 portrait wins | Short-form vertical video (like TikTok/Reels) outperforms horizontal talking-head clips. | Buffer |

### 2. Ideal Video Length

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet spot: 15-30s | Maximum impact for most content. Optimal watch time is 13-15 seconds. | SproutSocial |
| Upper limit: 10 min | Native video max is 10 minutes. Longer form works for complex/educational content. | Hootsuite |
| Under 15s for ads | Most successful video ads are under 15 seconds. | Hootsuite |
| 30-90s: upper funnel | Works for brand awareness and consideration goals. | Hootsuite |
| First 3s critical | Viewer attention drops sharply after 10 seconds — hook immediately. | SproutSocial, Hootsuite |

### 3. Hashtag Strategy

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Use 3-5 hashtags | Specificity over volume. Do NOT use the 3-3-3 rule (that's Instagram). | SproutSocial |
| Precision tools | Hashtags amplify strong copy but don't rescue weak copy. | SproutSocial |
| Keyword-first | LinkedIn algorithm is keyword-driven, not hashtag-volume-driven. Use industry keywords in caption. | SproutSocial |
| Mix: broad + niche + branded | Tailor hashtags to each post's topic. | SproutSocial |
| Capitalize multi-word | `#SocialMediaMarketing` for accessibility, not `#socialmediamarketing`. | SproutSocial |
| Post-2024: SEO signals | LinkedIn removed hashtag following, profile listings, dropdown search. Hashtags now act as SEO signals, not discovery tools. | SproutSocial |

### 4. Engagement Hooks

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hook in first 1-3s | Lead with a question, startling stat, or explicit value promise. | SproutSocial, Hootsuite |
| State what's coming | Explicitly tell viewers what they'll get by watching ("Here's how I..."). | Buffer |
| Essential info first | Attention drops after 10 seconds — put the payoff early. | Hootsuite |
| Ask questions | Questions in video + caption drive comments. | SproutSocial |

### 5. Caption Style

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Always add captions | 79% of LinkedIn videos are watched with sound off. Closed captions or on-screen text required. | SproutSocial |
| Keep copy short | 150 characters or fewer recommended for post body. | Hootsuite |
| Lead with keywords | Industry keywords in caption for LinkedIn SEO. Full sentences, not keyword-stuffed. | Buffer |
| Include a link | Drive website visits from the caption. | Hootsuite |
| Use "video" in text | Mentioning "video" in promotions increases click-through rates. | Hootsuite |
| Professional tone | Conversational but professional. Avoid jargon. One idea per paragraph. | General best practice |

### 6. Best Content Types

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 short-form portrait | TikTok/Reels-style vertical video performs best. Explore lifestyle/behind-the-scenes, not just talking-head. | Buffer |
| Thought leadership | Original perspectives, bold takes, industry hot takes. | SproutSocial |
| Behind-the-scenes | Company culture, day-in-the-life, work processes. | SproutSocial |
| Tutorials | Step-by-step guides, how-tos, educational walkthroughs. | SproutSocial |
| Testimonials & case studies | Customer success stories, client results. | SproutSocial |
| Product demos | Launches, demonstrations, feature highlights. | SproutSocial |
| Event highlights | Conference clips, live session recordings, event recaps. | SproutSocial |
| LinkedIn Live | Real-time connection, Q&A, AMA sessions — 23x more comments. | SproutSocial |

### 7. Posting Frequency & Timing

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Best days: Tue-Thu | Tuesday through Thursday have peak engagement. | SproutSocial |
| Best time: 8-9 AM | Tuesday and Wednesday mornings are optimal. | Hootsuite |
| Weekends: avoid | Lowest engagement days for LinkedIn. | Hootsuite |
| Consistency > volume | Post as often as quality can be maintained. | SproutSocial |
| Industry varies (examples) | Tech: 11 AM Mon. Finance: 1 PM Wed, 5-7 PM Thu. Healthcare: 9AM-1PM Tue-Fri. | Hootsuite |

## FACEBOOK

### 8. Community-Building Patterns

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Video drives group engagement | Use video inside Facebook Groups to spark discussions. Native video posts in groups get higher reach. | Buffer, SproutSocial |
| Host expert discussions | Use video (Live or recorded) for conversations with SMEs or customers. | SproutSocial |
| Feature UGC | Ask followers to submit videos, then share and credit them. | SproutSocial |
| Respond to all comments | Show the brand is engaged — reply promptly to every comment on video posts. | SproutSocial |
| Use polls with video | Pair video posts with a poll or question to double engagement. | SproutSocial |

### 9. Video Engagement Tactics

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hook in first 3s | First frame and first 3 seconds determine scroll-past vs watch. | Buffer, Later |
| Ask questions in copy | Direct questions in the post body drive comments. | Buffer |
| Tag relevant Pages | Tagging other business Pages increases cross-audience reach. | Buffer |
| Use Facebook Live | Live videos rank higher AND increase exposure of your non-live content. | Buffer |
| Add CTA in video | CTA in copy + mid-roll text overlay + end card. | Buffer |
| Invite longer-form thoughts | Ask open-ended questions ("What's your take?") not just yes/no. | Buffer |
| Mix formats | Alternate between video, Reels, Stories, and photo posts to keep feed dynamic. | SproutSocial |

### 10. Sharing Mechanics

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ALWAYS upload natively | Native videos: **109.67% higher interaction rate**, **477.76% higher share rate** than YouTube links. | Buffer |
| Never paste YouTube links | Facebook penalizes external video links in the algorithm. Re-upload native. | Buffer |
| Craft a searchable title | Descriptive video title improves discoverability in Facebook search. | Buffer |
| Give preview in copy | Tell people what they'll see before they click play. | Buffer |
| Embed on blog posts | Embedding Facebook videos on external blogs drives more reach back to Facebook. | Buffer |
| Nudge shares explicitly | Ask for a share in the caption or via on-screen text ("Share this if..."). | Common practice |
| Boost top-performing organic | Use Facebook's boost feature on organic posts that already have traction. | SproutSocial |

### 11. Ideal Video Length

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Native video: 30s-3min | Standard native video (horizontal) works well at 30 seconds to 3 minutes. | SproutSocial |
| Reels: under 90s | Higher completion rates under 90 seconds. | Later (Reels best practices) |
| First 3s make or break | Auto-play means the first frame and 3 seconds determine whether someone keeps scrolling. | Buffer |
| One key point per video | Focused, single-message videos are more watchable and shareable. | Buffer |
| Live: 10-30 min | Facebook Live sessions work well at 10-30 minutes for depth. | Buffer |

### 12. Caption & Description Strategy

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Captions on EVERY video | Silent auto-play is the default. Add closed captions or burned-in text. | Buffer |
| Colorful text overlays | Use bright, contrasting text on screen for key points (not just white text). | Buffer |
| Suggest "tap for sound" | A well-timed, non-disruptive pop-up telling viewers to unmute. | Buffer |
| Character limit: 2,200 | Full caption length limit. Lead with the hook. | Later |
| 3-5 hashtags | Relevant hashtags for discoverability. Not keyword-stuffed. | Later |
| Hashtags + emojis | Emojis add personality to captions. Use sparingly. | SproutSocial |
| Clear CTA | Every video caption should tell the viewer what to do next (comment, share, visit link). | Buffer, SproutSocial |
| First line is the hook | The first line of the caption shows before "see more" — make it compelling. | General best practice |

## Cross-Platform

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Captions required both | Both LinkedIn (79% mute rate) and Facebook (auto-play silent) need captions. |
| Native upload always | Both platforms penalize external video links vs native upload. |
| Hook early | First 1-3 seconds determine scroll-past vs watch on both platforms. |
| 9:16 for short-form | Both platforms now favor vertical short-form video. |
| Consistency beats volume | Post on a regular cadence rather than flooding and going silent. |
| One point per video | Single-message videos perform better than trying to cover multiple topics. |
