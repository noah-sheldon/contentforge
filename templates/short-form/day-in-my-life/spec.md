## Script formula

**30s:** Hook (0-3s) → 3 beats @ ~6s → payoff (24-27s) → CTA (27-30s)
**60s:** Hook (0-3s) → 6 beats @ ~8s → payoff (52-57s) → CTA (57-60s)

- Hook: premise + number or identity ("Day in the life of an AI lead at Fitch Ratings") — or tension hook ("my boss texted me at 7am"). Never "hey guys".
- Beats: one activity = one scene = 6-8s footage + 1-2s on-screen time label.
- Payoff: deliverable / result / reveal.
- CTA: serialization ("I'll be vlogging the build") > "follow me". Or question to comments / "part 2?".

## Beat sheet

1. Wake-up aesthetic (coffee, light) — 2 shots, 4-6s
2. Morning routine montage — 3-4 shots, fast
3. Commute + city b-roll — 2 shots
4. Desk/office core (40% of runtime) — screens, meetings, typing close-ups
5. Midday social — lunch, coffee run
6. Afternoon work — productivity peak
7. Evening wind-down + tomorrow preview
8. CTA card

## Music & SFX

- Lo-fi / indie-pop / phonk-lite, 90-120 BPM. Trending audio on TikTok = discovery vector.
- Energy: slow in → lift at office core → settle at wind-down.
- SFX: whoosh on EVERY cut (signature), camera shutter for stills, keyboard SFX on desk close-ups, soft pop on text.

## Cuts / transitions / zooms

- Shot length 1.5-2.5s; 3-4s only on emotional beats. 30s ≈ 15-20 shots.
- Hard cut + whoosh = default (no black dips). 1-2 match cuts per video. Whip-pan between locations.
- Punch-in (200-300ms) on: every text label, screen close-up, reveal. Slow push only on opening aesthetic shot.

## Text & animations

- Time labels ("7:30", "14:00") in clean sans — format signature lower-third.
- 1-line plain captions on spoken beats; silent montage runs text-free (music carries).
- Minimal VFX: warm/neutral grade protecting skin tone, optional film grain. No heavy motion graphics.
