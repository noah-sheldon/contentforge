Format: workspace tour / gear reveal. Vertical 9:16, IG Reels + TikTok. Music-only is the default; VO is the differentiator.

## Script formula

**30s (music-only or light VO):**
1. 0-3s hook over money shot — "This is my dream desk setup" or price-tension ("cost more than my first car")
2. 3-15s gear run — gear name + price labels, no sentences
3. 15-24s one "why" sentence — "Everything here is built for one job: shipping AI work without friction"
4. 24-30s total-cost reveal + CTA — "What should I upgrade next?"

**60s (short VO):** hook → 3-zone tour (display / input / compute+audio), one line per zone → cable-mess beat → final reveal → CTA. Max 6-8 spoken lines, Grade 5-6 plain speech.

## Beat sheet

1. Hook: full-desk money shot (or before/after split) — 1-2s
2. Wide establish of room — 1s
3. Zoom-in monitor (screen glow on) — 1s
4. Push-in keyboard (macro detail) — 0.5-1s
5. Push-in mouse + desk mat — 0.5-1s
6. Chair (sliding in) — 0.5-1s
7. Peripherals chain (mic, headphones, speakers, camera, lights) — 0.5-1s each
8. Cable-management payoff / desk underside — 1s
9. Full re-reveal, all on, slow push-in — 2-3s
10. Total-cost text card + CTA — 1.5-2s

## Music & SFX

- Cozy/modern: lo-fi or soft piano/synthwave 90-105 BPM. Battle-station hype: synthwave/EDM 110-128 BPM.
- SFX pillars: mechanical keyboard clicks, mouse click, unboxing foley on push-ins (ASMR), whoosh 0.3-1s on every transition, impact 0.2-1s on each reveal, riser 1-3s into final reveal.
- Levels: music under VO -20 to -30 dB; SFX -10 to -20 dB; final -14 to -16 LUFS.

## Cuts / transitions / zooms

- Short-form: 0.5-1.5s per shot (1-2 cuts/sec peak); never >3s except hook and final reveal.
- Match cuts between similar objects (signature). Whip pans between desk zones. Punch-in zoom (10-15% scale, fast-in settle) on each gear item — exactly one per item. Fade-through-white/black on aesthetic beats + reveal. Hard cuts elsewhere (no crossfades in fast sections).
- Final reveal = one long slow push-in (2-3s) across the whole desk.

## Text & animations

- Gear labels: 1-3 words, bold sans, name + price ("Keychron Q3 — £189"), 1.5-2s display.
- Price callouts = virality lever. "Total: £6,200" final card.
- Captions: white + black outline, 2-4 words/line, max 2 lines, cut-synced.
- Animation: clean pop-in + fade (100-200ms), subtle. No flashy motion graphics.

## VFX / grade

- Screen glow on monitors. Light-leak/bokeh foreground on aesthetic beats. Speed-ramp on punch-ins. Motion blur on whip pans (30-50%).
- Grade 5-10% cooler on TikTok (compression warms). Protect skin tone.

## IG vs TikTok

| | TikTok | IG Reels |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 21-34s | 45-60s slower cut |
| Pacing | 1-2 cuts/sec, re-hook every 5-7s | 2-4s per cut, editorial |
| Music | Trending EDM/hip-hop bias | Jazz/lo-fi "sophisticated" bias |
| CTA | Comment-bait ("what should I upgrade next?") | Save/share ("save this for your setup") |

Practical: post 30s fast-cut to TikTok, 45-60s slower VO-lite cut to Reels with a 150-char caption ending in a question.
