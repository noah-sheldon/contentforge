# Transformation — Short-Form Template

Format: before/after career or glow-up arc ("how I went from X to Y"). Vertical 9:16, IG Reels + TikTok. Maps to "from grad to AI leader" positioning.

## Script formula

**30s:** Hook (0-3s) → Before (3-10s) → Turning point (10-14s) → After (14-22s) → Lesson (22-27s) → CTA (27-30s)
**60s:** Hook → Before (5-7 shots + context) → Struggle/low point → Turning point (decision + "Day 1… Day 40" countdown) → After + proof (offer, title, numbers) → Lesson (2 bullets max) → CTA (follow + comment keyword)

- Hook: "I went from [X] to [Y] in [N] months" — said + shown in text. Or "wait for the transition".
- Before: struggle evidence, desaturated grade, dated text.
- Turning point: the single catalyst.
- After: result montage, warm grade, stat count-ups.
- Lesson: one concrete takeaway, ≤6 words on screen.
- CTA: "Follow for [repeatable value]" or comment keyword.

## Beat sheet

1. Before portrait (grayscale) → 2. Struggle montage → 3. Catalyst card → 4. Transition moment (money beat) → 5. After montage (warm) → 6. Proof card (stat) → 7. Lesson text → 8. CTA overlay.
- Serialized variant: "Day N" countdown cards instead of catalyst + after.

## Music & SFX

- Energy curve low → high. Ambient/piano over "before"; first real drop lands EXACTLY on the before→after transition.
- Slowed + reverb trending tracks work for visual montages.
- SFX: whoosh/impact on the transition frame, punch at each stat count-up, riser 1s before the drop.

## Cuts / transitions / zooms

- Before montage: 1-1.5s shots (jittery). After montage: 1.5-2.5s (breathing room). Fast-cut cluster right before turning point, then one ~2s hold on the transition frame.
- THE money moment: split-screen wipe (before left / after right) OR match cut on pose/gaze/angle, timed to the beat drop. Secondary: 1-2 frame flash at the drop; whip-pan between timeline beats ("2022 → 2026"). Hard cuts elsewhere — no dissolves.
- Punch-in (105-115% over ~0.3s) on each struggle beat and stat card; slow push-in during "after" reveal.

## Text & animations

- "BEFORE" / "AFTER" corner labels (before = grey, after = brand/white).
- Dates as progress proof: "2022 → 2026", "Day 1 → Day 40" countdown ticker.
- Stat count-up animation (salary, followers, applications).
- Timeline/roadmap bar that fills across the video.

## VFX / grade

- Color-grade shift IS the core VFX: desaturated/cool before → warm/saturated after.
- Speed ramp on transition frame, animated diagonal swipe for split, subtle grain on "before".

## IG vs TikTok

| | TikTok | IG Reels |
|---|---|---|
| Length | Up to 10 min; longer storytelling OK | Up to 3 min, Explore-eligible |
| Distribution | Discovery-first, trend-driven | Relationship-weighted: saves/shares/repeats feed it |
| CTA | Comment keyword (no auto-reply on TikTok) | Comment → DM automation ("comment PLAN") native |
| Audio | Massive library + user sounds | Meta Sound Collection; audio doesn't carry over |
| Rule | — | Penalizes TikTok-watermarked content (Originality Score) — re-record per platform |

Net: TikTok gets the raw, trend-native, longer cut first; IG gets a tighter, polished, DM-funnel cut. Same footage, two cuts, never a watermarked re-upload.
